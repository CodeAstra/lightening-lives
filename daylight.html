We design genetic tests the other way round

Genetic tests have traditionally needed large equipment, specialised laboratories and trained staff, so they rarely reach the people who need them most. We start from the other end: tests designed to be simple, affordable and usable outside specialised laboratories, so molecular diagnosis can reach underserved communities.

What sets our tests apart

See the REASSURED approach

Simple sample collection

A dried blood spot: a few drops of blood on a card. Simple and minimally invasive to collect.

Extraction-free

No separate DNA extraction step.

Quick results

Rapid turnaround, so programmes can act on results sooner.

Simple to run

Designed so that collecting, processing and reading results doesn’t call for specialised training, large equipment or a specialised laboratory.

Built for scale

Affordable and scalable, with high-throughput screening and an automation-friendly workflow for large public health programmes.
