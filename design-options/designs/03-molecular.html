The company

The company

Lightening Lives is a research-oriented and innovation-driven molecular diagnostics organisation1, focused on rapid, affordable, scalable, extraction-free, highly accurate diagnostic solutions2 for various genetic disorders that reach even the most underserved communities3.

Lightening Lives LLP is based in Hyderabad. It develops extraction-free, blood-card-based genetic screening kits and supplies them to laboratories, hospitals, clinicians and government and public-health programmes, who run the assay themselves.

1

A diagnostics company, not a testing service

Lightening Lives LLP develops and supplies kits. Laboratories, hospitals and clinicians run the assay themselves. Testing is done in-house only for research collaborations.

2

Extraction-free is the mechanism

A punch from the dried blood spot goes straight into the assay. Removing the extraction stage is what makes the one-drop specimen, the turnaround and the throughput possible.

3

Built to reach beyond the laboratory

Cards are collected by non-specialists in the field and travel without a cold chain, so a screening programme can reach districts with little laboratory infrastructure.

How it works

How it works

Specimen to result in four steps.

  1. 01

    Collect one drop

    A finger-prick drop of blood goes onto the card. Anyone can collect it, as with a routine blood-sugar test.

  2. 02

    Dry on the card

    The blood spot dries on the card. The card is labelled and sent to the laboratory.

  3. 03

    Run the extraction-free assay

    A punch from the card goes straight into the assay. There is no DNA extraction step.

  4. 04

    Result in under 24 hours

    The laboratory reports in under 24 hours. Current capacity is 1,000 samples in under 24 hours.
