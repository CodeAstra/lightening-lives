About Lightening Lives

Lightening Lives is a research-oriented and innovation-driven molecular diagnostics organisation, focused on rapid, affordable, scalable, extraction-free, highly accurate diagnostic solutions for various genetic disorders that reach even the most underserved communities.

Lightening Lives LLP is based in Hyderabad. It develops extraction-free, blood-card-based genetic screening kits and supplies them to laboratories, hospitals, clinicians and public-health programmes, who run the assay themselves.

About the company

One drop. One day.

Specimen to result in four steps. The step that is missing is DNA extraction, and that is the point.

Photo: Gannu03, CC BY-SA 4.0

  1. Collect one drop

    A finger-prick drop of blood goes onto the card. Anyone can collect it, as with a routine blood-sugar test.

  2. Dry on the card

    The spot dries on the card, which is labelled and sent to the laboratory.

  3. Run the extraction-free assay

    A punch from the card goes straight into the assay. There is no DNA extraction step.

  4. Result in under 24 hours

    The laboratory reports in under 24 hours.

Why Lightening Lives

Extraction-free is the technical differentiator. It is what makes one drop, a result in under 24 hours and high throughput possible.

  • 1 drop

    One-drop blood-based test

    A dried blood spot on a card. Anyone can collect it, as with a routine blood-sugar test.

  • Extraction-free genetic testing

  • 24 h

    Rapid turnaround
