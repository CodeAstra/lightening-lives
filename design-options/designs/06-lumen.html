Company

Lightening Lives is a research-oriented and innovation-driven molecular diagnostics organisation, focused on rapid, affordable, scalable, extraction-free, highly accurate diagnostic solutions for various genetic disorders that reach even the most underserved communities.

Lightening Lives LLP
Hyderabad, India

Lightening Lives LLP is based in Hyderabad. It develops extraction-free, blood-card-based genetic screening kits and supplies them to laboratories, hospitals, clinicians and government and public-health programmes, who run the assay themselves.

Method

Why Lightening Lives

Extraction-free is the technical differentiator. It is what makes a one-drop sample, a turnaround under 24 hours, minimal laboratory infrastructure and high throughput possible.

  • Specimen

    One-drop blood-based test

    A dried blood spot on a card. Anyone can collect it, as with a routine blood-sugar test.

  • Method

    Extraction-free genetic testing

    A punch from the card goes straight into the assay. There is no DNA extraction step.

  • Turnaround

    Rapid turnaround

    Reports in under 24 hours.

  • Access

    Affordable and accessible testing

    Removing the extraction step removes its time, cost and equipment. Contact us for pricing.

  • Infrastructure

    Minimal laboratory infrastructure required

    No extraction step means fewer instruments, fewer consumables and fewer manual stages.

  • Throughput

    High-throughput screening

    Current capacity: 1,000 samples in under 24 hours.

  • Automation

    Automation-friendly workflow

    Few manual steps, so the workflow transfers readily to automated platforms.

  • Scale

    Designed for large-scale public-health programmes

    Card collection by non-specialists in the field, with bulk supply and programme support.

Workflow

How it works

Specimen to result in four steps.

  1. 1Step 1 of 4

    Collect one drop

    A finger-prick drop of blood goes onto the card. Anyone can collect it, as with a routine blood-sugar test.

  2. 2Step 2 of 4

    Dry on the card

    The blood spot dries on the card. The card is labelled and sent to the laboratory.

  3. 3Step 3 of 4

    Run the extraction-free assay

    A punch from the card goes straight into the assay. There is no DNA extraction step.

  4. 4Step 4 of 4

    Result in under 24 hours

    The laboratory reports in under 24 hours. Current capacity is 1,000 samples in under 24 hours.

Compared with a conventional workflow

A conventional workflow adds DNA extraction between specimen and assay — lysis, binding, washing, elution — each with its own reagents, instruments and hands-on time. The extraction-free workflow goes from card punch to assay.
