Who we are

Lightening Lives is a research-oriented and innovation-driven molecular diagnostics organisation, focused on rapid, affordable, scalable, extraction-free, highly accurate diagnostic solutions for various genetic disorders that reach even the most underserved communities.

Why Lightening Lives

Eight plain facts

  • One-drop blood-based test

    A dried blood spot on a card. Anyone can collect it, as with a routine blood-sugar test.

  • Extraction-free genetic testing

    The assay runs directly from the card. No DNA extraction step, no extraction equipment.

  • Rapid turnaround

    Reports in under 24 hours of the sample reaching the laboratory.

  • Affordable and accessible

    Priced for screening at population scale. Contact us for a quote.

  • Minimal laboratory infrastructure

    Runs in a basic molecular laboratory — no extraction line required.

  • High-throughput screening

    Current capacity: 1,000 samples in under 24 hours.

  • Automation-friendly workflow

    The card-based format slots into automated, batch-based workflows.

  • Built for public-health programmes

    Designed for large-scale screening across districts and states.

Products

Seven kits

  • Sickle Cell AnaemiaICMR certified

    Screening and diagnosis from a single dried blood spot. Developed and commercialised under an MOU with CCMB.

  • Thalassaemia

    Carrier and patient screening for alpha and beta thalassaemia.

  • Spinal Muscular Atrophy

    Screening for SMA in symptomatic children and at-risk couples.

  • Duchenne Muscular Dystrophy

    Screening for DMD, including maternal carrier testing.

  • Haemophilia

    Screening for haemophilia and related coagulation disorders.

  • Pharmacogenomics

    Metaboliser status to inform treatment and dosing decisions.

  • Custom Panels

    Custom screening panels for programmes and research collaborations.

Kits are supplied to laboratories, hospitals and screening programmes — not to individuals.

How it works

Four steps

  1. Collect one drop

    a finger-prick drop of blood, collected by any trained worker — no phlebotomist required.

  2. Dry on the card

    the spot dries on the card and travels by ordinary post — no cold chain.

  3. Run the assay

    the extraction-free assay runs directly from the card in a basic molecular laboratory.

  4. Result in under 24 hours

    a clinical report is returned within 24 hours of the sample reaching the laboratory.
