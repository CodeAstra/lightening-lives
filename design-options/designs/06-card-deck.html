Who we are

Lightening Lives is a research-oriented and innovation-driven molecular diagnostics organisation, focused on rapid, affordable, scalable, extraction-free, highly accurate diagnostic solutions for various genetic disorders that reach even the most underserved communities.

Why Lightening Lives

Eight cards on the table

One-drop blood-based test

A dried blood spot on a card. Anyone can collect it, as with a routine blood-sugar test.

Extraction-free testing

The assay runs directly from the card. No DNA extraction step, no extraction equipment.

Rapid turnaround

Reports in under 24 hours of the sample reaching the laboratory.

Affordable and accessible

Priced for screening at population scale. Contact us for a quote.

Minimal infrastructure

Runs in a basic molecular laboratory — no extraction line required.

High-throughput screening

Current capacity: 1,000 samples in under 24 hours.

Automation-friendly

The card-based format slots into automated, batch-based workflows.

Built for programmes

Designed for large-scale public-health screening across districts and states.

How it works

Four cards, dealt in order

Step 1

Collect one drop

A finger-prick drop, collected by any trained worker — no phlebotomist required.

Step 2

Dry on the card

The spot dries on the card and travels by ordinary post — no cold chain.

Step 3

Run the assay

The extraction-free assay runs directly from the card in a basic molecular laboratory.

Step 4

Result in under 24 h

A clinical report is returned within 24 hours of the sample reaching the laboratory.
