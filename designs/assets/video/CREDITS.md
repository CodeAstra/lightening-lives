## Changes made to the originals

- All three: trimmed, scaled down from 1080p, lightly denoised and re-encoded without sound.
- Parts 1 and 2: colour temperature warmed a little, to take the blue cast off the laboratory light.
- Parts 2 and 3: slowed to five-sixths speed (30 frames a second played at 25).
