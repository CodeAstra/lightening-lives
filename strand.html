See the evidence

Reading a gene used to take a specialised laboratory.

Genetic tests have traditionally needed large equipment, specialised laboratories and trained staff, so they rarely reach the people who need them most. We design ours the other way round.

  1. Collect

    A few drops of blood on a card. A dried blood spot is simple and minimally invasive to collect.

  2. Process

    No separate DNA extraction step. The workflow is automation-friendly, suits high-throughput screening and needs minimal laboratory infrastructure.

  3. Read

    Results come back quickly, and our tests are designed so that reading them doesn't call for specialised training.

The result is genetic testing that is scalable and affordable enough for large public health programmes.

Our flagship · Sickle cell anaemia

LitLife™-SCA

A simple dried blood spot test for detecting sickle cell anaemia, developed for the National Sickle Cell Anaemia Elimination Mission.

  • Validated by ICMR
  • MOU with CSIR-CCMB
  • Dried blood spot sample

In India, sickle cell disease is most common among tribal communities. The Government of India launched the Elimination Mission in July 2023, aiming to eliminate the disease as a public health problem by 2047.

  • The condition you need next

    Tell us what your programme needs to screen for. We develop the test, to the same REASSURED standard as the five here.

    Tell us what you need

  • Thalassaemia

    Carriers are usually healthy and often unaware of their status. Genetic testing supports carrier screening, diagnosis and family counselling.

  • Spinal muscular atrophy

    Treatments work best when started early, ideally before symptoms appear. Our dried blood spot test is adaptable and affordable for large-scale newborn screening.

  • Duchenne muscular dystrophy

    Diagnosis often comes late. An earlier genetic diagnosis allows timely care, and lets families find out whether relatives are carriers.

  • Blood coagulation disorders

    In bleeding disorders such as haemophilia, genetic testing confirms the diagnosis, identifies carriers and supports family counselling and care planning.

Nine more letters: the standard every test is held to.

REASSURED is a published set of criteria for diagnostic tests that work in low-resource settings. Developing genetic tests that meet it is what we do best.

  1. Real-time connectivityReal-time connectivity

    Results can be read or shared digitally with decision-makers.

  2. Ease of specimen collectionEase of specimen collection

    Samples are simple and minimally invasive to collect.

  3. AffordableAffordable

    Affordable for the people tested and for the health system.

  4. SensitiveSensitive

    Few false negatives.

  5. SpecificSpecific

    Few false positives.

  6. User-friendlyUser-friendly

    Simple to perform, with minimal training.

  7. Rapid and robustRapid and robust

    Quick results; tests survive transport and storage without special conditions.

  8. Equipment-free or simpleEquipment-free or simple

    No special equipment, or only simple devices.

  9. Deliverable to end-usersDeliverable to end-users

    Reaches the people who need it most.

SourceLand et al., Nature Microbiology (2019). REASSURED updates the earlier ASSURED criteria from the World Health Organization's tropical disease research programme.
