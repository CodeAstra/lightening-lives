Questions we are asked.

Do you test individuals?

No. We work with hospitals, laboratories, clinicians, NGOs, CSR funders and government health programmes. If you are looking for a test for yourself or your family, please speak to your doctor or visit your nearest government health facility.

What does our laboratory need to run the kits?

A thermal cycler, a gel electrophoresis tank and a punch, a bench, and staff who have run PCR or have been trained. No proprietary system. We help set the method up and train your staff.

How do samples reach the laboratory?

On the card, at room temperature, by ordinary courier or post. No cold chain.

Are the kits approved?

LitLife™-SCA, our sickle cell kit, is approved by ICMR, which validated it with 100% sensitivity and specificity. The technology behind our kits is patented in India and Japan.

How quickly are results ready?

The same day. Many cards run together, and screening and confirmation come from the same run.

Can you develop a test for a condition that isn't listed?

Yes. Each test we have developed began with an institution that needed it. Tell us the condition and the setting, and we take it from there.
