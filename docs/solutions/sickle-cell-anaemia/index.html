Sickle cell anaemia, screened and confirmed in one test.

LitLife™-SCA reads the sickle mutation directly from a dried blood spot. One result separates patients, carriers and unaffected people, from birth onwards.

  • ICMR approved
  • INJPPatents granted in India and Japan
  • From birth
About the condition

Sickle cell anaemia is inherited when a child receives the sickle haemoglobin gene (HbS) from both parents. It causes pain crises, infections and anaemia, and is common in tribal communities of central, western and southern India. A child diagnosed early can be protected with timely care.

A person with one copy of the sickle gene is a carrier, usually healthy and often unaware; when both parents are carriers, each pregnancy has a 1 in 4 chance of the disease.

LitLife™-SCA. What it detects.

One test screens and confirms, so a positive result needs no second test.

  • The sickle mutation

    Detects the sickle mutation in the HBB gene.

  • Three results

    Unaffected, carrier (sickle cell trait) or patient (sickle cell disease).

  • From birth

    A newborn can be tested at birth. A DNA test does not depend on the type of haemoglobin a newborn makes.

  • For programmes and families

    Population screening, newborn screening, antenatal and family testing.

Who it is for

  • State and district health programmes screening for sickle cell disease

  • Hospitals and laboratories that confirm a diagnosis

  • Newborn screening programmes in the sickle cell belt

  • NGOs and CSR funders running screening in tribal districts

  • Sickle-beta thalassaemia

    LitLife™-SiBThal217 also identifies the compound state, in which one parent passes on HbS and the other a beta thalassaemia mutation. On routine tests it is often read as sickle cell disease or as trait.

  • HbD

    HbD Punjab can look like HbS on some routine methods, and HbS/HbD causes disease. Testing for HbD is available.

Why a DNA test here. Where today's testing stops.

  1. 1

    The solubility test shows that sickle haemoglobin is present, but cannot tell a carrier from a patient.

  2. 2

    HPLC confirms, but needs a venous sample, a cold chain and a central laboratory, and is not reliable in newborns.

  3. 3

    Every positive screen needs a second visit, and many people never return.

In the laboratory, with the kit.

  • The sample

    A finger-prick spot of blood, dried on a card. Cards keep and travel at room temperature.

  • The method

    Mutation-specific PCR with no DNA extraction, read on an agarose gel.

  • The equipment

    A thermal cycler, a gel electrophoresis tank and a punch.

  • Controls

    Controls on every run.

  • Where it is made

    In India, by a third-party manufacturer that holds a quality certification.

Can it tell sickle cell trait from sickle cell disease?

Yes. It reads both copies of the gene, so one test shows whether a person has no sickle gene, one (a carrier) or two (a patient).

Can newborns be tested?

Yes, at birth. A DNA test does not depend on the type of haemoglobin a newborn makes, which is what limits HPLC in the first days.

Does the sample need a cold chain?

No. The dried card travels at room temperature.

Does a positive result need confirmation by another test?

No. LitLife™-SCA is a screening and confirmatory test.
