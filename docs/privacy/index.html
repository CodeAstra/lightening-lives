Privacy

What this website collects, why, and how to reach us about it.

Who we are

This website is published by Lightening Lives LLP, D-306, Sy. No. 117, Indu Aranya Pallavi, GSI (SR), Bandlaguda, Hayathnagar, Hyderabad, Telangana 500068, India.

What we collect

  • What you send us. If you fill in the enquiry form or write to us, we receive your name, organisation, role, email address, your phone number and state if you give them, the conditions you ask about, and your message. The form also notes which page of this website you opened it from.
  • The enquiry form. Our enquiry form is run for us by Fillout (fillout.com), which receives what you type into it on our behalf and passes it to us. Its page loads only when you open the form.
  • What the server records. Like most websites, ours is hosted by another company, whose servers record the internet address a request comes from, the page asked for and the time.
  • Analytics. We do not use analytics or advertising trackers.

What we do not collect

We do not collect health information through this website. Please do not send patient names, test results or medical records through the enquiry form or by email. If your enquiry involves patient information, tell us, and we will agree a secure way to share it.

Why we use it

To reply to your enquiry and, if a collaboration follows, to work with your institution. We do not sell personal information or use it for advertising.

Who sees it

The members of our staff who handle enquiries; Fillout, which runs our enquiry form; and the companies that host our website and email. We disclose it to anyone else only where the law requires.

Your choices

You can ask what we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, or tell us to stop contacting you. Write to our Grievance Officer at admin@lighteninglives.in or at the address above.
