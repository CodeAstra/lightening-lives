# Image credits

The photographs here are representative images, chosen under the plan's selection rules (`docs/superpowers/plans/2026-10-24-website-build.md`, Phase 3): places the company has worked, laboratory work, the material of the method and care settings, with nobody identifiable. They are labelled as places or subjects and never captioned as the company's work. Replace them with Lightening Lives' own photographs as they arrive (`content/website-content.md`, Part 7.2 and 5.11 "The photographs").

The stills of the card, the batch and the map (`site-*.webp`) and the preview card (`social.png`, `social/*.png`) are not photographs and need no credit: they are rendered from the company's own card and map in `src/js/site/scene.js` and the page itself.

The originals are in `src/images-src/` (at most 2400 px on the long side). `npm run images` writes each at three widths, `{basename}-640.webp`, `{basename}-1280.webp` and, when the picture is at least 1920 px wide, `{basename}-1920.webp`, and turns the table below into `src/site/_data/credits.json`, from which the Terms page's "Photo and map credits" lists them. Keep the table's seven columns: the script reads them. "Footer label" is the short name the footer gives the picture; "Changes" is shown after the credit in brackets (`-` for none beyond resizing).

## Changes made to the originals

- All photographs: resized to at most 2400 px on the long side for `src/images-src/`, then to the published widths, and converted to WebP; metadata removed.
- `place-kothagudem`: cropped from about 5:4 to 3:2 (part of the sky removed) before it was saved to `src/images-src/`.
- `lab-gel`: cropped to 16:10 before it was saved to `src/images-src/`, to leave out the scientist's face above the gel; only the gloved hands remain.
- `method-paper`: the left 16% cut away, which carried part of the screening card's printed serial number (a sample code; content file 5.11).
- The `method-paper` crop is made by `scripts/images.mjs` (`CROPS`), so its original in `src/images-src/` is uncropped; the `place-kothagudem` and `lab-gel` files there are already cropped.

## What each picture is not

Each picture stands for a place or a subject, never for the company's work. None shows a patient or a programme of the company's; the place photographs show a district or a city the company has worked in, or a place near one in the same state (Tamhini Ghat for Nandurbar, Ooty for Gudalur, Ahwa in Dang for Valsad), and are labelled with the place's name only. `care-newborn` was taken in the USA and `care-anganwadi` in a Chhattisgarh village: they show a kind of place, and are captioned by what they show, not as the company's sites.
