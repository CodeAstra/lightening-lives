From the field

Three accounts from our work with institutions. Details that could identify a family have been left out.

  • Madhya PradeshDuchenne muscular dystrophy

    A diagnosis at three

    A three-year-old boy in a village in Madhya Pradesh was losing strength in his muscles. His family, a long way from any specialist hospital, took it for poor nutrition. An NGO working in the area arranged a molecular test, and the result was Duchenne muscular dystrophy.

    What it led toShow less

    With a diagnosis at three, the family could begin supportive therapy and join a patient support group, instead of spending years without an answer.

  • ChhattisgarhSpinal muscular atrophy

    Two carriers, one plan

    A couple in Chhattisgarh had lost their first child to spinal muscular atrophy without ever learning the cause. During their second pregnancy they were referred to us through a government screening programme. Carrier testing showed that both parents carry the SMA mutation.

    What it led toShow less

    With counselling and early testing they could plan with full information. They now have a healthy child.

  • OdishaSickle cell anaemia

    What had been called fate

    In a village in Odisha, a young couple had watched two children in their extended family live with severe anaemia and repeated infections. Testing showed that both of them carry the sickle cell gene.

    What it led toShow less

    In their village the illness had been put down to fate. The couple now know what it is, how it is passed on and what their choices are.
