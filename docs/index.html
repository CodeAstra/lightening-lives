Innovate. Patient, carrier or unaffected, read in one run.

Mutation-specific PCR, read on an agarose gel, separates the three in a single test. Screening and confirmation happen together, from a dried blood spot.

  • Screening cum confirmatory. No second test and no second visit.

  • From birth. Newborns can be tested at birth.

  • No cold chain. Dried cards travel at room temperature.

  • No DNA extraction. A punch from the card goes straight into the reaction.

How the test works

Diagnose. What changes when you switch.

Transform lives. Six steps, from a finger prick to a family in care.

A test only matters if its result reaches care. Our testing model follows each sample all the way, with counselling and support for every family.

Testing

Care

  1. 1 Collect

    A finger-prick sample on a card, in the community or at birth.

  2. 2 Transport

    To the nearest testing centre, at room temperature.

  3. 3 Test

    Extraction-free molecular testing with LitLife™.

  4. 4 Report

    Results recorded against each sample in the app.

  5. 5 Treat and counsel

    Treatment, with genetic and social counselling for patients and carriers.

  6. 6 Follow up

    Awareness programmes and patient support groups.

Map summary. Sickle cell disease is most common in a belt across central India, running from south-eastern Gujarat through Madhya Pradesh, Maharashtra and Chhattisgarh to south-western Odisha.

The National Sickle Cell Anaemia Elimination Mission covers 17 focus states: Gujarat, Maharashtra, Rajasthan, Madhya Pradesh, Jharkhand, Chhattisgarh, West Bengal, Odisha, Tamil Nadu, Telangana, Andhra Pradesh, Karnataka, Assam, Uttar Pradesh, Kerala, Bihar and Uttarakhand.

Thalassaemia: found across India, where on average 3 to 4 people in 100 are carriers; higher carrier frequencies are reported in Punjab, Delhi, Gujarat, Maharashtra and West Bengal.

Where we have worked: population and newborn screening at 15 sites in nine states and Delhi: Delhi, Rajasthan, Madhya Pradesh, Jharkhand, Chhattisgarh, Maharashtra, Gujarat, Telangana, Karnataka and Tamil Nadu.
