Turn any basic PCR room into a high-throughput genetic lab.

For standalone labs, lab chains and hospital laboratories.

What changes for your laboratory

  • Zero DNA extraction. A tiny punch from the card goes straight into the PCR tube: no extraction step, hours saved.

  • Zero cold chain. Cards arrive at room temperature in ordinary envelopes from remote centres and clinics, with no leaking tubes or haemolysed samples to reject.

  • One test instead of two. Screening and definitive molecular confirmation in a single run.

  • High throughput. Dozens of cards in one run, with same-day turnaround.

  • Reports clinicians can act on. Affected patient, carrier (trait) or unaffected, without ambiguity.

What your laboratory needs

A thermal cycler, a gel tank, a card punch, and technicians who have run basic PCR or whom we train.

Removing the three biggest bottlenecks in public health screening.

Two-step screening loses patients, spends budget on cold chains and misses newborns. LitLife™ fixes all three.

  • People lost between screening and confirmation. One test screens and confirms from the first finger-prick spot, so no family travels back for a second visit.

  • A cold chain from village to laboratory. Cards need no refrigeration: health workers send them from remote sub-centres to a district laboratory by ordinary post or courier.

  • Newborns discharged before testing. LitLife™ reads the DNA from a heel-prick spot at birth, with no 72-hour wait.

How a programme runs

Testing

Care

  1. 1 Collect

    A finger-prick sample on a card, in the community or at birth.

  2. 2 Transport

    To the nearest testing centre, at room temperature.

  3. 3 Test

    Extraction-free molecular testing with LitLife™.

  4. 4 Report

    Results recorded against each sample in the app.

  5. 5 Treat and counsel

    Treatment, with genetic and social counselling for patients and carriers.

  6. 6 Follow up

    Awareness programmes and patient support groups.

From testing to complete care.

Your support delivers high-impact healthcare: a confirmed answer, counselling and care for every family.

Why it matters

  • Every rupee reaches a definitive answer. Screening that stops at a possible case leaves families in limbo; one confirmatory test gives a life-changing answer at low cost.

  • A camp that explains and tests. Awareness and genetic screening run together, collecting finger-prick spots while educating the community.

  • Families are never left alone with a result. Every patient or carrier family is counselled, referred for prenatal diagnosis where needed, and linked to clinical care.

Questions funders ask

We are an NGO or foundation with no laboratory. Can we still run a screening programme?

Yes. Your field volunteers or health workers collect a finger-prick spot on our barcoded card. The dried cards travel by ordinary courier to our laboratory, or a regional partner laboratory, for testing and reporting.

Do you help CSR funders design measurable programmes?

Yes. We design end-to-end genetic health programmes with CSR teams and foundations, aligned with national priorities such as the National Sickle Cell Anaemia Elimination Mission and maternal and child health.

What happens to the children and carrier families a programme finds?

Diagnosis is the beginning of care. Every patient is linked to local clinical treatment, and carrier couples receive genetic counselling and prenatal diagnostic support to plan future pregnancies.
