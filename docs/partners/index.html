Our partner network

Who we work with across science, philanthropy and community care.

Thirteen organisations so far: the scientific institutions behind our tests, the foundations that fund screening, and the patient groups that bring it to families.

Science, R&D and incubation. Where the tests come from.

Built on know-how and a formal collaboration with the CSIR-Centre for Cellular and Molecular Biology (CCMB), Hyderabad. Our sickle cell test is approved by ICMR.

  • CSIR-Centre for Cellular and Molecular Biology
  • Atal Incubation Centre, CCMB
  • Nucleome

Foundations and CSR. Who funds the screening.

CSR initiatives and foundations fund last-mile screening, newborn testing and nutritional-genetic care across underserved tribal and rural belts.

  • Tata Trusts
  • Tata Steel Foundation

Patient and community organisations. Who brings it to families.

NGOs, sickle cell and thalassaemia societies and neuromuscular support groups build trust, run awareness camps and counsel families with us.
