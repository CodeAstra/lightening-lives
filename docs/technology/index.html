The method. Six steps, nothing hidden.

Mutation-specific PCR and agarose gel electrophoresis, methods taught in every molecular biology course. What we invented is how they work together on a dried spot.

  1. Step 1: Spot

    A finger-prick sample is spotted on a barcoded card and dried.

  2. Step 2: Punch

    A small disc is punched from the spot into a PCR tube.

  3. Step 3: Mix

    A ready reaction mix specific to the mutation is added.

  4. Step 4: Amplify

    PCR runs in a standard thermal cycler, many cards in one run.

  5. Step 5: Separate

    The product is run on an agarose gel.

Compared. One test where there were two.
