How it works

Faster, simpler, affordable, accessible, scalable.

That is how we sum up our technology. Here is what stands behind each word.

Faster
No separate DNA extraction step, and a rapid turnaround from sample to result.
Simpler
A dried blood spot is simple and minimally invasive to collect. The tests are designed so that collecting, processing and reading them doesn't call for specialised training.
Affordable
Designed to be affordable for the people tested and for the health system.
Accessible
Minimal laboratory infrastructure: no large equipment or specialised laboratory needed, so molecular diagnosis can reach underserved communities.
Scalable
An automation-friendly workflow for high-throughput screening, designed for large-scale public health programmes.

The standard

REASSURED, criterion by criterion.

A published set of nine criteria for diagnostic tests that work in low-resource settings. Developing genetic tests that meet them is our distinguishing strength.

  1. R

    Real-time connectivity

    Results can be read or shared digitally with decision-makers.

  2. E

    Ease of specimen collection

    Samples are simple and minimally invasive to collect.

  3. A

    Affordable

    Affordable for the people tested and for the health system.

  4. S

    Sensitive

    Few false negatives.

  5. S

    Specific

    Few false positives.

  6. U

    User-friendly

    Simple to perform, with minimal training.

  7. R

    Rapid and robust

    Quick results; tests survive transport and storage without special conditions.

  8. E

    Equipment-free or simple

    No special equipment, or only simple devices.

  9. D

    Deliverable to end-users

    Reaches the people who need it most.

Source Land et al., Nature Microbiology (2019). REASSURED updates the earlier ASSURED criteria from the World Health Organization's tropical disease research programme.

Who we work with

Institutions, and the programmes they run.

Most of our work begins as a collaboration. Tests are supplied in bulk, to institutions and government programmes.

  • Hospitals, laboratories and clinicians

    Genetic tests for inherited conditions, designed to need minimal laboratory infrastructure and to suit an automated workflow.

  • District health officers and government programmes

    Tests developed for national health programmes, for screening whole populations.

  • NGOs and CSR funders

    A technology partner for large, population-level screening.

Awareness and counselling, alongside the tests

Awareness programmes explain inherited conditions and screening to communities. Counselling helps people and families understand results and carrier status. We run both, mostly on behalf of the government.
