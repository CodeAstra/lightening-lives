## Changes made to the originals

- All images: resized and converted to WebP; metadata removed.
- `field-kothagudem`: cropped to 16:10 (part of the foreground shore and sky removed).
- `field-jharkhand`: cropped to 16:10 (roadside kerb at the bottom edge removed).
- `sickle-cells`: cropped from portrait to 4:3.

## Licence notes

- CC BY and CC BY-SA images need a visible credit (author, licence, link to source) wherever they are published. A "Photo credits" line or link in the site footer that points to this list meets that.
- The edited CC BY-SA files above remain under CC BY-SA 4.0. This applies to the image files only, not to the rest of the website.
- The CDC asks that its public domain images are credited to CDC and the photographer, with the CDC Public Health Image Library named as the source.
